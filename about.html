WHY IT EXISTS

Legal careers shouldn't require an inside connection.

The legal profession can feel distant to students who haven't had the opportunity to meet attorneys or learn what different legal careers actually look like. The Legal Lens was created to close some of that gap.

Through direct conversations, educational resources, and live Q&As, the initiative gives students a way to hear honest career stories and ask questions directly.
